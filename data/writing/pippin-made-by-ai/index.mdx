[Pippin](https://th1nhng0.itch.io/pippin) is a short point-and-click game I made for a game jam. Nell gets her grandmother's old cabinet. Each of its five drawers holds a tiny living model of a place, and a slider moves that place through time.

I didn't write any of it. Claude Code wrote the code, the story and the dialogue. The voices and music came from ElevenLabs. The whole repo history is five days long.

## There are no image files

My first question was where the art came from. There is no art folder. Every pixel is placed by code.

Each scene is built out of colored points in 3D: boxes, roofs, cylinders, tree canopies. The farm alone is 66,414 points. Here it is drawn point by point, in the order the code adds them:

<LoopVideo webm={buildWebm} mp4={buildMp4} width={596} height={380} label="The farm diorama assembling itself: grass, then the soil block, the pond, the field, the orchard, the barn and house, and finally the people and animals" />

A point on its own is just one color. The look comes from a few cheap tricks stacked on top of each other: each material has one base color, each face of a box gets darker the more it faces away from the light, and every point picks a slightly different shade from its material at random. Things that move, like the cows, the windmill and falling leaves, are drawn fresh every frame on top.

<LoopVideo webm={layersWebm} mp4={layersMp4} width={644} height={520} label="The farm drawn in four steps: flat colors, then darker shading on the sides, then grainy texture on every surface, then the animated parts like cows, the windmill and falling leaves" />

To decide which point is in front, the renderer keeps a second image next to the colors: how far away each pixel is.

## Time is one number

Every scene is a function of one number, the slider, from 0 to 1. On the farm, that number times 12 is the month. Trees, snow, crops and the sky all read it, so moving the slider is just drawing the same scene again with a different number.

<LoopVideo webm={sliderWebm} mp4={sliderMp4} width={644} height={536} label="The farm with a slider under it. As the slider moves from 0 to 1, the month label runs from March to February and the farm goes through blossom, summer, harvest and snow" />

## Hints that point somewhere

Stuck players get a Hint button in three steps: a nudge, then where to look, then an arrow on the exact thing. A test plays the whole game and checks after every step that the hint points at something, so it can never send you nowhere.
